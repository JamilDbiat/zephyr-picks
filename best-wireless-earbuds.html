What actually matters

Spec sheets love to bury the two things you'll care about most: fit and battery. Everything else is secondary. If a pair is uncomfortable after an hour, no amount of frequency response will save it. And if you're charging the case every other day, you'll end up leaving them at home.

When we compare earbuds, we weigh five things in roughly this order:

  • Fit and comfort — the difference between wearing them all day and taking them out after a meeting. Look for multiple ear-tip sizes in the box.
  • Battery life — aim for 6+ hours per charge and 24+ hours with the case. Anything less and you're planning your day around a charger.
  • Noise handling — active noise cancelling (ANC) is worth it for commutes and open offices. A good "transparency" mode matters just as much for walking near traffic.
  • Call quality — the spec nobody checks until their first bad call. Multiple microphones and wind reduction make a real difference.
  • Controls and app — reliable touch or button controls and an app that lets you remap them and tweak EQ.

How to choose between them

Start with how you'll use them. If you're on calls all day, prioritise the microphone and multipoint pairing. If you commute on public transport, ANC is the feature that changes your daily experience the most. If you're buying your first pair or a gym backup, the budget option gives you 80% of the experience for a fraction of the cost.

One last tip: whatever you buy, try all the included ear tips. The right seal transforms both comfort and sound — most people who say earbuds "don't fit them" are simply using the wrong size.

The bottom line

For most people, the all-rounder is the safe, satisfying choice. Save with the budget pick if features aren't a priority, and step up to the premium pair only if earbuds are a big part of your day. Prices change often, so check the current listing before you buy.
